## Our story

**Serve All Solutions Limited** is a Lilongwe-based technology and event solutions
company dedicated to revolutionizing how Malawians experience live football. Through
our secure online ticketing platform, **SASMW.com**, we bring convenience,
transparency, and excitement to every match day.

Founded in Lilongwe, Malawi, we saw a simple problem: buying a football ticket
should not mean long queues, cash-only stalls, or uncertainty. We built a platform
where any fan — whether in Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu, or anywhere in the country —
can buy a match ticket online in seconds with **Airtel Money**, and walk into the
stadium with nothing more than a QR code on their phone.

## Mission

To make football accessible to every Malawian by delivering fast, secure, and
affordable online ticketing and technology solutions that fans and clubs trust.

## Vision

To be Malawi's leading sports and events ticketing platform, and a catalyst for
growing live football culture across the nation.

## Our values

- **Innovation** — We use modern technology to solve real local problems.
- **Accessibility** — Everyone should be able to attend a match, from any phone.
- **Integrity** — Transparent pricing, honest service, and secure payments.
- **Community** — We invest in the football community that supports us.

## Why choose SAS?

- **Secure Payments** — Pay safely with Airtel Money (Airtel Malawi).
- **Instant E-Tickets** — Get your ticket by email immediately after payment.
- **QR Code Entry** — Scan and walk in; no printing needed.
- **24/7 Support** — Our team is always ready to help fans and partners.
