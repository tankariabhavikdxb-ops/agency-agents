## 1. When you can get a refund

**Cancelled events**
If a match is cancelled and not rescheduled, you are entitled to a refund of the
ticket price you paid, minus any non-refundable platform/processing fees.

**Rescheduled events**
If a match is postponed/rescheduled, your ticket is generally automatically valid
for the new date. If you cannot attend the new date, you may request a refund
within **7 days** of the postponement announcement.

## 2. When you cannot get a refund

- Once a match has been **played**.
- If you simply change your mind or no longer wish to attend.
- If your ticket is voided due to a breach of the Terms & Conditions (e.g.,
  unauthorised resale).
- For lost or misused e-tickets.

## 3. How to request a refund

1. Email **support@sasmw.com** with your **order number** and the match details.
2. Include the email address / phone number used at purchase for verification.
3. Our team will confirm eligibility and process the refund.

## 4. Refund method & timing

- Refunds are returned to the **Airtel Money** account used (or to the payment
  method available) within **5–10 working days** after approval.
- Amounts are refunded in **Malawi Kwacha (MWK)**.

## 5. Partial refunds / fees

- Where platform or processing fees apply, they are deducted from the refund as
  disclosed at purchase.
