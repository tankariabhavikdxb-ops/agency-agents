## How to buy tickets

**Q: How do I buy a match ticket on SASMW.com?**
Browse the Matches page, select your match, choose your ticket category (VIP,
Standard, or General), select the quantity, go to checkout, enter your details, pay
with Airtel Money, and confirm the payment prompt on your phone. Your e-ticket with
a QR code is emailed to you instantly.

**Q: Do I need an account to buy a ticket?**
No. You can buy as a guest. Creating a free account lets you view your order history
and re-download tickets.

**Q: Which ticket categories are available?**
VIP, Standard, and General. Prices and availability are shown on each match page.

**Q: Can I buy tickets for someone else?**
Yes. Enter the correct name/email/phone for the ticket holder during checkout so
the e-ticket reaches the right person.

## Payment (Airtel Money)

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
Currently **Airtel Money (Airtel Malawi)**. We plan to add more options in future.

**Q: How does Airtel Money payment work?**
After checkout you enter your Airtel Money number. Airtel sends a USSD prompt to
your phone — enter your PIN to confirm. Once confirmed, your payment is complete.

**Q: What if I don't receive the payment prompt on my phone?**
Check that you entered the correct +265 number and that your phone has network.
If you don't get the prompt within a couple of minutes, contact support@sasmw.com
before the order times out.

**Q: Are payments secure?**
Yes. Payments are processed through Airtel Money's secure system. We never see or
store your PIN.

## E-ticket & QR code

**Q: Where do I find my e-ticket?**
It is emailed to the address you entered at checkout, from tickets@sasmw.com.
You can also download it from My Account → Orders if you created an account.

**Q: How do I enter the stadium with my ticket?**
Show the QR code on your phone at the gate (a screenshot works offline), or print
the PDF ticket. Gate staff scan it to let you in.

**Q: My email didn't arrive. What should I do?**
Check your spam/junk folder, then contact support@sasmw.com with your order number.

**Q: Can I transfer my ticket to a friend?**
E-tickets are generally non-transferable, but in some cases you may request a
transfer by contacting support with your order details.

## Refunds

**Q: What is your refund policy?**
If a match is cancelled, you are entitled to a full refund (minus any platform fees).
Once a match has been played, tickets are non-refundable. Refund requests for
rescheduled matches are handled case-by-case. See the Refund Policy page.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
Refunds are processed within 5–10 working days back to your Airtel Money account.

## Accounts

**Q: How do I create an account?**

**Q: I forgot my password.**
Use the "Lost your password?" link on the login page to reset it.
