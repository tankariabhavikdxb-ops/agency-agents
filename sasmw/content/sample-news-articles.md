## Article 4 — Top 5 Football Stadiums in Malawi

- **Category:** Local Football
- **Featured image suggestion:** Stadium collage.

Malawi has a growing football culture and some venues that can create unforgettable
match-day experiences. Here are five stadiums every football lover should know.

1. **Bingu National Stadium (Lilongwe)** — The country's biggest and most modern
   venue, home of the national teams and major finals. Capacity ~40,000.
2. **Kamuzu Stadium (Blantyre)** — The legendary home of Malawian football and the
   derby, with a famously passionate atmosphere.
3. **Civo Stadium (Lilongwe)** — A well-used venue for Super League matches in the capital.
4. **Mzuzu Stadium (Mzuzu)** — The northern hub for football, hosting regional fixtures.
5. **Chilomoni / community grounds** — Grassroots venues where the next stars begin.

### Plan your match day
Whatever stadium you visit, get your ticket in advance on **SASMW.com**. Skip the
queue, buy with Airtel Money, and walk straight in with your QR e-ticket.
