## 1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how **Serve All Solutions Limited** ("we", "us", "our")
collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use **sasmw.com** and
purchase tickets.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Contact details:** name, email address, phone number (including your Airtel
  Money number).
- **Order information:** match, ticket category, quantity, price, order ID.
- **Account information:** if you register — username, password (stored hashed),
  order history.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser/device type, pages visited (via analytics),
  to improve the service.

## 3. How we use your information

- To process and deliver your ticket orders and e-tickets.
- To communicate with you about your order (including payment and ticket confirmation).
- To provide customer support.
- To send marketing/updates **only if you have opted in**, and you may opt out anytime.
- To improve our website, security, and services.

## 4. Payment information

- Airtel Money payments are processed by **Airtel Malawi**. We do **not** store your
  Airtel Money PIN or full payment credentials on our servers.
- We share only the minimum information (order reference, amount, your Airtel number)
  needed to process your payment.

## 5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only:
- With payment partners (Airtel Money) to process transactions.
- With service providers who help operate the platform (hosting, email, analytics),
  under confidentiality obligations.
- Where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## 6. Data retention

We keep order records as long as needed for legitimate business and legal/tax
purposes. Account data is kept while your account is active.

## 7. Your rights

You may:
- Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
- Ask us to correct inaccuracies.
- Request deletion of your data, subject to legal/record-keeping obligations.
- Opt out of marketing communications.

To exercise any right, contact **privacy@sasmw.com** or **info@sasmw.com**.

## 8. Cookies

We use essential cookies (e.g., for your cart/login) and, with your consent,
analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can control cookies in your browser.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data,
including HTTPS, secure storage, and restricted access. No method of transmission
is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 10. Data protection & law

We handle personal data in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements
in the Republic of Malawi and treat privacy seriously.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on
this page, with the updated date shown below.
